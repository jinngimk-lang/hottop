# Hottop Meme

## Recovery first

Inside this repository, read `PROJECT.md` and `STATUS.md` first. For creative strategy, **REQUIRED:** apply `brand-metaphor-creative` before locking the joke or output format. This skill handles hotspot acquisition/evidence and the four-panel path when four-panel is actually the best form.

## Hotspot discovery

Prefer current, recognizable topics with a clear visual conflict or cultural action. Collect enough evidence to know what the topic is and why it is current; do not turn a headline fragment into a factual claim.

Prioritize:

- recognizability;
- freshness;
- conflict/role clarity;
- visual potential;
- fit with the promoted subject's real pain point/differentiator;
- source quality.

A trend is raw material, not the concept. If the product-to-hotspot bridge is forced, choose a different trend.

## Comparison handling

If a rival is not supplied, research direct competitors, incumbents, substitutes, legacy workflows and manual workarounds. Pick the clearest pain-point contrast rather than the most famous name.

Named-brand negatives require a supported limitation, accurate category/design tradeoff, a job the rival is not designed to solve, or unmistakable subjective satire. Never invent performance, price, outages, defects, safety failures or sentiment. Otherwise use a generic category proxy or make the old assumption the antagonist.

## Four-panel path

Use only when narrative escalation materially improves the idea:

1. **Setup** — recognizable hotspot world + user friction.
2. **Escalation** — old approach / comparison embodies the friction.
3. **Reversal** — promoted subject changes the outcome through its real differentiator.
4. **Punchline** — short visual resolution + subject/phrase.

Do not force the product into a hero costume. It may be the route, prop, tool, transformation or reveal if that is more natural.

## Visual medium router

Match the way people recognize the hotspot without copying protected production assets:

- **Film/live action:** original photorealistic cinematic staging in the broad genre; no actor likeness, exact frame, official poster, exact costume/prop/set.
- **Animation:** original 2D/3D/low-poly treatment matching the broad animation grammar; no protected character replica.
- **Real people/social phenomena:** documentary/social-native realism with anonymous people unless rights-cleared user assets are supplied.
- **Technology/software:** realistic contemporary tech environments; prefer physical metaphor over copied proprietary UI.
- **Food/consumer products:** polished commercial product imagery when product texture/material is central.
- **Native memes:** reproduce the distribution grammar, not source pixels or protected character design.

## Copy and claim rules

Default to concise Chinese when the active campaign is Chinese. Captions must work on a phone. Objective claims such as `10x faster`, `cheapest`, `safer than X`, `X cannot do Y`, market leadership or numeric performance require evidence. Otherwise mark the concept `satire` or `needs_evidence`.

## Archive contract

A ready hotspot concept should retain:

- promotion context;
- topic, timestamp and evidence;
- comparison candidates / chosen target;
- bridge and creative reframe from `brand-metaphor-creative`;
- selected format;
- visual medium / genre treatment;
- beats, captions and punchline;
- image prompt + exclusions;
- risk flags and claim status.

If another format (single metaphor, swipe-reveal, split old-vs-new, product-as-prop, faux still/poster) communicates the idea better, route back to `brand-metaphor-creative` instead of forcing four panels.
