# PersonaShot narrative-continuity admission — 2026-08-28

## Why this matters

Production v0.2 already separates **identity fidelity** from **requested-action / motion fidelity**. A fresh benchmark paper, **PersonaShot: Benchmarking Person-Centric Narrative Continuity in Multi-Shot Video Generation** (arXiv:2608.16717, published 2026-08-17), exposes another useful boundary: attractive shots can still fail as a narrative when physical state resets across cuts, affect/emotion changes incoherently, or cinematic relations break.

The paper reports roughly 1,000 multi-shot segments and 16 metrics spanning three families:

- **physical continuity**;
- **affective dynamics**;
- **cinematic grammar / shot relations**.

It evaluates continuity at within-shot, cross-shot-transition, and sequence-trajectory levels and reports that perceptual quality can diverge materially from narrative continuity.

Primary paper: https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.16717

## Admission result

**Status: research-only evaluator-design signal.**

As of 2026-08-28, targeted public GitHub/web searches did not locate an official PersonaShot code/data repository or an independently reviewable source/dataset/evaluator license surface. Therefore Hottop does **not** import benchmark code/data, claim PersonaShot-calibrated thresholds, or add a new evaluator dependency.

This is an evaluation-design admission, not a generation-backend admission.

## Useful Hottop consequence

For future real multi-shot operator benchmarks, do not let the existing identity + motion evidence dimensions silently stand in for complete narrative continuity. When the story requires them, preserve separate evidence for:

1. **identity fidelity** — is the same subject still recognizably the same subject?
2. **requested-action / motion fidelity** — did the subject perform the requested action rather than merely move?
3. **physical-state continuity** — do persistent objects, poses, injuries/transformations, carried props, scale and spatial relations remain causally consistent across cuts?
4. **affective continuity** — when emotion/performance is part of the brief, do expression and emotional trajectory remain coherent with the story rather than reset arbitrarily?
5. **cinematic relation continuity** — do eyelines, screen direction, shot ordering, geography and transition logic remain coherent?

These dimensions may share evidence artifacts but must not be collapsed into one generic “consistency” score. A future evaluator must identify its revision and criterion semantics, and output claims must remain bound to exact reference/plan/generated bytes and generator provenance under the existing Hottop artifact rules.

## What this does not change

- It does not change the guaranteed software3d route.
- It does not add a model, GPU dependency, hosted service, credential, paid call or automatic download.
- It does not weaken the current `hottop.reference-continuity-benchmark.v1` identity/motion gates.
- It does not claim PersonaShot code, data, evaluators, thresholds or weights are available or licensed for Hottop use.
- It does not justify provider repinning or a new backend abstraction.

## Re-admission gate

Revisit implementation only if an official/reviewable release appears. Before importing or adapting anything, independently verify code license, dataset rights, evaluator/checkpoint license, runtime requirements, hidden download/network behavior and commercial/geographic restrictions. Prefer provider-neutral criterion contracts or independently implemented behavior when that avoids unnecessary heavyweight dependencies.
